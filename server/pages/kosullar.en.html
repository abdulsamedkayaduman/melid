Terms of use and privacy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short
  • You can use the Melid app without an account. This text only covers the optional account and sync service.
  • Your connections, passwords and keys are end-to-end encrypted on your device. The server cannot read them.
  • The server only keeps your username, email, encrypted vault, device information and (optional) usage counts.
  • Even without an account, the app reports its version and platform with a random installation ID when checking for updates (to see how many devices use it; can be turned off).
  • You can delete your account from the app at any time; everything is deleted immediately.

1. The service

Melid is an open-source (MIT-licensed) SSH terminal and SFTP file manager for Linux, Windows, macOS and Android. The sync service at melid.abdulsamed.net is offered free of charge to sync your sessions between your devices and is run personally by Abdulsamed Kayaduman.

The service is provided "as is"; uninterrupted operation and freedom from data loss are not guaranteed. Make your own backups of important information with the app's Backup feature. If the service is ever discontinued, this will be announced on the home page in advance; your data stays on your devices.

3. Information we don't collect

  • Server addresses, usernames, passwords, keys: all of these are inside the encrypted vault and are never sent with usage counts.
  • What you type in the terminal, the commands you run, or the names or contents of files you transfer. Your SSH connections go directly from your device to your own servers; they don't pass through this service.
  • Location, contacts, advertising IDs. The web pages use no cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising (a language preference cookie is set only if you switch the language).

Your information is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties. The server is located in the data center hosting the service; traffic is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS) through Cloudflare.

4. Retention and deletion

Your information is kept until your account is deleted. In the app, use Account and sync → Delete account with your account password: account data, the encrypted vault, recovery data, device sessions and usage counts are deleted immediately, and your username is removed from the daily statistics. Sessions on your devices are not deleted.

Accounts that are abused or unused for a long time (more than a year) may be deleted.

5. Security and your responsibility

  • The encryption key is derived from your account password on your device. If you forget your password, nobody can open your vault. Create a recovery key in the account window and keep it somewhere safe.
  • Don't share your account password or recovery key with anyone.
  • Don't use the service for illegal purposes, to access other people's systems without permission, or in a way that overloads the server. Accounts found to be abusing the service may be deleted.

6. Your own server

The sync server is open source and consists of a single file. If you don't want your information stored elsewhere, run your own server and enter its address in the app: github.com/abdulsamedkayaduman/melid.

7. Changes and contact

If this text changes, the current version is published on this page with the date of the change. If the information collected expands, the app will tell you separately. For questions and requests: write to us on GitHub.
